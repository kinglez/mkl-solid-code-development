# Implementation Brief — Draft

This template is a draft, not confirmation of requirements or completed verification. Adapt it to the task's scope. Update an existing specification where appropriate.

## Objective and scope

Describe the actors, goals, delivery scope, and whether the request is for design, implementation, or review.

## Confirmed rules and sources

Record rules, authoritative sources, and the basis for confirmation. Keep proposals, assumptions, and open questions separate from confirmed requirements.

## Acceptance scenarios

Connect concrete inputs, observable outcomes, and rules. Cover relevant normal, boundary, and failure behavior.

## Necessary design and trade-offs

Explain responsibilities, interface contracts, dependencies, and where failures stop progress. Justify new abstractions with actual variation, costs, and limitations. Select robustness, static, interaction, or state views according to the question being answered.

## Implementation slices and verification entry points

Split work into verifiable business outcomes. State actual dependencies and public verification entry points. Derive verification commands from project configuration.

## Open decisions and implications

List unresolved questions, recommendations, dependent work, and independent work that can continue.

## Actual delivery and verification

After implementation, record changed locations, commands actually run and their results, review corrections, unexecuted checks, and limitations. Distinguish evidence of business behavior from evidence about contracts or structure.
